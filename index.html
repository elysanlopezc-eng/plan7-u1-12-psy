Montano Habits: Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood (respecting cultural perspectives) & Think Win-Win (designing ethical marketing strategies).

Stage I: Retrieval Practice (~5 Minutes)

Quick retrieval challenge bridging Plan #6 (Biological, Behavioral, Cognitive, Psychodynamic) with today's new perspectives.

Retrieval Check 1

Which perspective focuses on neurotransmitters, hormones, and physiological brain triggers?

The Biological Perspective.
Retrieval Check 2

How does Cognitive Psychology explain why consumers compare product specifications?

It views humans as information processors who use memory schemas and logical evaluation to make decisions.
Retrieval Check 3

What gap is left when we ignore culture, personal growth, and evolutionary survival?

We miss how social identity, cultural norms, self-actualization, and ancestral adaptations influence choices!

Stage II: Direct Instruction - 3 Perspectives & Drivers (~30 Minutes)

15 min Explaining Humanistic, Sociocultural & Evolutionary Perspectives | 15 min Needs, Values, Identity & Social Influence

1. Humanistic

Growth & Free Will

Focus: Rogers & Maslow. Human potential, free will, self-actualization, personal values, and authenticity.

Market Trigger: Products framed around personal growth, wellness, and achieving full potential.

2. Sociocultural

Culture & Conformity

Focus: Vygotsky & Asch. Social norms, cultural context, peer influence, social identity, and traditions.

Market Trigger: Social proof, trend adoption, community belonging, and cultural rituals.

3. Evolutionary

Adaptation & Survival

Focus: Darwinian principles. Natural selection, status signaling, mate attraction, resource security, and threat avoidance.

Market Trigger: Highlighting safety, dominance, status symbols, and scarcity.

Interactive Driver Identification:
"A teenager purchases a specific sneaker brand because all their classmates wear it to fit in."
Sociocultural Perspective (Normative Social Influence & Conformity)

Stage III: Culture & Belonging in Consumer Preferences (~15 Minutes)

Analyzing how cultural values and group belonging shape brand loyalty and market acceptance.

Collectivist vs. Individualist Markets

Individualist Cultures: Ads emphasize personal autonomy, standing out, and unique self-expression (Humanistic alignment).

Collectivist Cultures: Ads highlight family harmony, group benefit, and shared tradition (Sociocultural alignment).

Evolutionary Status Signaling

Why do luxury brands thrive across diverse cultures?

From an Evolutionary perspective, expensive goods act as costly signals of wealth, power, and genetic fitness, enhancing social rank within a group.

Stage IV: Ethical Marketing, Stereotypes & Activity 3 Workshop (~10 Minutes)

5 min Discussion on Stereotypes & Ethics + 5 min Exit Ticket & Activity 3 Launch

Ethics & Stereotypes in Advertising

Sociocultural and Evolutionary triggers are powerful, but misusing them can reinforce harmful stereotypes or exploit psychological vulnerabilities.

Unethical Trap: Harmful Stereotyping

Reducing cultural groups or genders to oversimplified tropes to trigger quick emotional sales.

Ethical Standard: Montano Win-Win Marketing

Respecting cultural diversity, empowering personal growth, and avoiding fear-based evolutionary exploitation.

Workshop Checkpoint

Activity 3 Workshop: Deconstructing Campaign Stereotypes

In pairs, analyze your assigned print/digital ad. Identify sociocultural cues and redesign any unethical stereotypes.

5-Minute Exit Ticket Prompt (Individual Submission)

Write 1 sentence: "How can a marketer leverage the Sociocultural perspective ethically without falling into cultural stereotyping?"
